# 2026-11-19 — feature session (issue #49: watch-list invariant for 2WL, part of #26)

- `core/src/lib.rs`: `dpll` returns `Some(a)` as soon as propagation over the watches ends without a
  conflict on a total assignment; the final full `propagate` check and the function `propagate` are
  gone. The `clause_status` re-check in `visit_slot` stays (it also covers clauses whose watched
  literal is not false, which the invariant does not exclude for an arbitrary call). `urust/`
  regenerated: 0 placeholders, no new stubs, locale unchanged; `check-spec.sh` ok.
- New `proof/QFBV_Watch.thy` (pure): the linked lists as `lseg nx s xs`; `wl_lists cs m p nx h Ls`
  (the lists of the first `m` clauses hold exactly the slots watching each literal, distinct, and the
  two watches of a clause are at different positions); `WS cs p t S` (a watch false by `S` has a
  true partner on the trail), `WP` (the same for a prefix of the trail, kept for every decision
  level) and `SP` (clauses of fewer than two literals are satisfied). Proved: `watch_init` builds
  the lists (`wl_init_step`, `wl_init_short`), moving a watch keeps them (`wl_move`), one step of
  `visit_slot` keeps the visit invariant `vinv` (`vinv_step`), so a visit without conflict extends
  `WS` by the false literal (`vinv_exit`) and every visit keeps `WP` at the earlier levels;
  `wpropagate` keeps it to the fixpoint (`wprop_winv_step`); decisions and chronological
  backtracking keep `wstate` (`wstate_decide`, `wstate_backtrack`); and `wstate_total`: a total
  assignment reached without a conflict satisfies every clause. `tpass_SP`: the first pass
  satisfies the short clauses.
- `proof/QFBV_DpllPure.thy` (the pure part of the old `QFBV_Dpll`, unchanged) and
  `proof/QFBV_DpllWatch.thy` (contracts up to `visit`, split out so the rest could be checked on a
  heap): `find_watch` now states that all other literals are false when it finds none,
  `move_watch` gives its exact result `mw_m`, `visit_slot` its step relation `vslot_post`,
  `watch_init` returns `wl_ok`, `visit` and `wpropagate` the invariant results (`visit_post`,
  `wprop_wpost`). `proof/QFBV_Dpll.thy`: the search loop carries `wstate`; `dpll_top_contract` and
  `check_sat_correct` are unchanged. The leaf-check lemmas are removed, `dpll_propagate_spec` too.
- Learned clauses: `wl_lists` is stated for any number of clauses and `wl_init_step` is exactly
  "append a clause watching two of its literals at the list heads". The one code change CDCL will
  need is the list end: links end at `next.len()`, which grows when slots are appended; a constant
  sentinel above every length (e.g. `usize::MAX`) avoids that.
- Pitfalls: in `apply (all ‹(rule r; assumption)?›)` style steps, `assumption+` after `rule` can
  succeed while leaving premises open (a wrong rule variant then left `False` goals); `;` may handle
  a premise before its schematic variables are fixed — use `rule r, assumption, ...` in order.
  `crush_base` unfolds definitions given in `simp add:` in the hypotheses too (`dpll_wpre`), so the
  helper lemmas must take the unfolded facts. The Pool Server re-checks the whole file after every
  edit (2–5 min per round at load ~30); splitting finished contracts into a theory on a task heap
  made the last rounds fast. Large goal states from `isabelle_goal` cost many tokens.
