## Model

- `clear_loop` (the loop that clears `set`), `copy_loop` (the copy loop over
  `trail[0..min nc (len trail)]`, where a trail literal is the pair `(var, neg)`), and
  `copy_phases_m = copy_loop (clear_loop tph set 0) [] trail nc 0`.
- `rephase_kind k` (the choice of `kind`), `rph_loop` (the loop over `ph`), and
  `rephase_m ph bph k = rph_loop ph bph (rephase_kind k) 0`.

## Lemmas and the obligations of PR #133

- **(a) `tph` keeps length `n`**: `copy_phases_m_props`: `length tph' = length tph`; every element
  of `set'` is `< length tph`; `length set' ≤ min nc (length trail)`. This needs no assumption
  on `set`, because both loops guard every index (as PR #133 says). `copy_phases_m_set` gives the
  closed form `set' = filter (λv. v < length tph) (map fst (take nc trail))`, and
  `copy_phases_m_mem` gives the matching membership condition.
- **The invariant**: `copy_phases_m_inv` assumes `tph` is `None` outside `set` and proves
  - `tph'` is `None` outside `set'`;
  - for every `v` in `set'` there is a last trail position `j < min nc (length trail)` with
    variable `v`, and `tph' ! v = Some (¬ neg)` at that position;
  - for any such last position `j`, `tph' ! v = Some (¬ snd (trail ! j))`.

  `copy_phases_m_distinct` is the simpler form for a trail with distinct variables.
  `copy_phases_m_zero` covers `nc = 0`: the result is all `None` and records nothing.
- **(b) `rephase` keeps `length ph`**: `rephase_m_props(1)`. The closed form of each entry is
  `rephase_m_props(2)`: `True` for kind 0, `False` for kind 1, and for kind 2 `bph ! i` if
  `i < length bph` and the entry is `Some`, else `ph ! i`. `bph` needs no length assumption.
  `rephase_kind_range` gives `rephase_kind k ∈ {0,1,2}`, and `rephase_kind_schedule` gives the
  schedule (0, 1, then [2,0,2,1] by `(k-2) mod 4`).
- **(b) Overflow**: `rephase_schedule_bound`: if `r < 2^20`, then `r + 1`, `1000 * (r + 1)` and
  `1000 * (r + 1 + 1)` are all `< 2^64`. The last one is `1000 * (rephased + 1)` after
  `rephased += 1`. A text block says that `total + …` still needs the bound on `total` of #108.
