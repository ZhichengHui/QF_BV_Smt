# 2026-11-27 — feature session (issue #88: tie-break direction flag in the pure decision-heap model; part of #26)

## Done

- `proof/QFBV_Heap.thy`: `before_m act rv u v ⟷ act ! u > act ! v ∨ (act ! u = act ! v ∧ (if rv then v < u else u < v))`.
  `rv` is threaded through `up_loop`, `heap_up_m`, `down_loop`, `heap_down_m`, `rebuild_loop`,
  `heap_rebuild_m`, `heap_insert_m`, `heap_pop_m`, `heap_pick_m`, `back_loop`, `heap_back_m`,
  `bump_loop`, `heap_bumped_m`, always directly after `act`.
- Every lemma keeps its statement, with `rv` as an extra free (universally quantified) variable.
  The proofs needed no change beyond the extra argument in `induction`/`case`/`simps[of …]` and the
  extra component in the `termination` measures.

## Decisions

- **Argument position.** The issue's text writes `before_m rev act u v`, but also asks to follow the
  position the prototype `2aafd23` uses. There `rev` comes directly after `act` in every function
  (`before(act, rev, u, v)`, `heap_up(heap, hp, act, rev, i)`, `heap_pick(heap, hp, act, rev, a)`, …).
  The model already puts `act` first, so `rv` goes directly after it. Then the model keeps the
  Rust's relative order of the arguments.
- **Name.** `rev` is the list-reversal constant of `Main`; a free variable named `rev` does not
  type-check, so the model calls it `rv`.
