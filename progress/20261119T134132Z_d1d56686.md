- `core/src/lib.rs`, section "Elimination of unconstrained definitions" (**not called by `solve`
  yet**): `elim(q) -> (q2, kind, mark)` and `reconstruct(q, kind, mark, q2, m2)`, helpers `bump`,
  `count_occ`, `term_width`, `ref_index`, `bit_const`, `free_once`, `free_kind`, `consumed`, `second`,
  `copy_op`, `copy_term`, `mark_live`, `bits_one`, `invert`, `var_index`, `settle`. A definition is
  *free* (kind 1-4) if it is a variable occurring once, or `Not`/`Extract`/`Add`/`Xor`/`Eq` (operand
  width > 0)/`Ite` (branches distinct width-1 constants) of a free operand referred to once
  (consumed), or `Mul`/`Udiv` of two distinct such operands (the second is marked 2 and set to 1).
  Free definitions of kind >= 2 become `Ref a` or `Extract(w-1, 0, Ref a)`; definitions unreachable
  from the assertions become `Const` zeros. `reconstruct` evaluates `q2` (`eval_defs`), then in
  increasing order evaluates non-free definitions of `q` and pushes the value of each free
  non-consumed definition (a *top*) down its chain of consumed operands (`settle`/`invert`, the
  other operand evaluated with `eval_term`), setting the variable at the bottom.
