## Verdict: correct; nothing blocks merge

- **Faithfulness**: statement by statement, `mk_b_m` / `norm_loop_b` / `normalize_b_m` match `mk` /
  `normalize_k` of `844a971`, and `bcost` / `ccount` match `bcost` / `term_ccount` (every op code,
  `Ite` on the then-branch width, first-operand width). The two stated deviations are harmless:
  saturation never changes a decision while `Σccount < 2^56` and `f ≤ 128` (a saturated `extra`
  is refused by both); the substituted `Var` definitions charge 0 in the model, so `seen`/`spent`
  agree at every step. Other divergences named: `w` as operand width (equal by `cwt`), the cap 16
  (modelled exactly, gives the `+15`), `extra` charged in full on reuse (same in the model;
  `ccs` bound is `≤`), `f = 0` (`usize` underflow in Rust vs `0` in HOL; irrelevant for `f = 2`),
  buckets not modelled (as in `QFBV_Norm`).
- **Strength**: `normalize_b_correct` = `normalize_d_correct` for all `f`; `pcount` uses each
  problem's widths as `fb_hyps`. The proof gives a bound with no hypothesis on `f`,
  `pcount q' ≤ pcount q + (f - 1) * Σccount(defs q)` (`normalize_b_ccount_le`). I checked it in a
  child session and offered it in the review (not pushed to the PR).
- **Correction to "What remains"**: `fb_hyps q'` does not follow from `fb_hyps q` alone (`pcount`
  may double past `2^56`). It follows from `sizes_ok p` through the 4× slack of `ob_arith` in
  `lower_fb_hyps` (`pcount < 2^54`). The `tbound` conjunct needs a short argument: new
  definitions have `tbound ≤ max 5 (2w+2)`, which is not monotone. This is a follow-up of about
  150–250 lines.
- **Refactoring suggested** (follow-up): derive `ninv_d_step` / `ninv_step` from `ninv_step_of`;
  factor the shared ~40-line tail of the three `normalize*_correct` theorems into one lemma on
  `ninv`; inline `len_charge`; rename one of the two `mul_step`s.
