# 2026-11-19 — feature session (issue #37: two-watched-literal unit propagation, part of #26)

- `core/src/lib.rs`: unit propagation over two watched literals per clause. The watch lists are
  flat linked lists in three `Vec<usize>` (`pos[s]`: position of the literal slot `s` watches, slot
  `2c + k` = watch `k` of clause `c`; `next[s]`; `head[lit_index(l)]`), so no new type of mutable
  local and no change of the locale. `watch_init` watches the first two literals of every clause of
  at least two literals; `wpropagate` visits, for each trail entry from position `q` on, the list of
  its negation (`visit`, slot by slot `visit_slot`): a clause whose other watch is true stays, else
  the watch moves (`find_watch`, `move_watch`), else `clause_status` of the clause decides unit /
  conflict. `dpll` runs one `propagate_pass` first (unit clauses), then the watches; on a total
  assignment the old full `propagate` checks every clause before `Some` is returned (a conflict
  there backtracks like any other). Backtracking sets `q` to the flipped entry; watches are not
  touched on backtrack. `urust/` regenerated: 0 placeholders, no new stubs, locale unchanged.
- Proofs (`proof/QFBV_Dpll.thy`): the watches are *not* trusted. Every implied literal and every
  conflict found through them is confirmed by `status_m` of its clause (`wprop_post`: new entries
  implied, conflict only if real), and completeness at the leaf comes from the final full check
  (`dprop_post` / `dpll_quiet`). So the watch-list invariant of the issue's deliverable 2 is not
  proved; it is not needed for `check_sat_correct` (unchanged). Index bounds of the watch arrays
  come from their lengths (`dpll_wpre`); the slot loop is bounded by a step counter
  (`steps < next.len()`), which never cuts a well-formed list short. Contracts: `lit_index`,
  `watch_init`, `find_watch`, `set_at`, `move_watch`, `visit_slot`, `visit`, `wpropagate`, `dpll`
  (new precondition `2 * |cs| <= fuel`, `2 * |cs| < 2^64`, `2 * n < 2^64`).
  `fuel_bound` (`QFBV_Defs`, mirrored by `fb_set` in `QFBV_Refine`) gains the element
  `2 * (vars + clauses)`; `fuel_bound_lt`, `fuel_bound_le` (`QFBV_Bound`) and
  `fuel_bound_ge`/`solve_core_spec` (`QFBV_Proof`) are extended accordingly.
